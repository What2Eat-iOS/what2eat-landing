Our Story - Making Food Transparency Accessible

A simple solution to a complex problem: understanding what's in our food and making informed choices

The Problem We Solved

Food labels are confusing and often misleading. Hidden ingredients, complex nutritional information, and unclear health impacts make it difficult for consumers to make informed choices about what they eat. Many people struggle with dietary restrictions, allergies, or health goals but lack the tools to navigate the complex world of food labeling.

Our Innovative Solution

What2Eat simplifies food labels through advanced technology. Our smart food scanner app instantly analyzes any product to provide clear, understandable information about ingredients, nutritional value, and health impact. We help users make better food choices through personalized insights and comprehensive allergen detection.

Meet Our Team

As passionate student developers from SRM University, Himavarshith Reddy, Sumanaswi Akkisetty, and Maneeth Reddy developed What2Eat to make healthy eating simpler and more accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

We're committed to making food transparency accessible to everyone. Whether you're a parent concerned about your family's nutrition, a health-conscious individual tracking your diet, or someone with dietary restrictions, What2Eat empowers you to make informed choices about what you eat.
